# Screening Checklist / 湾区租房筛选清单

Use this checklist before touring, applying, or signing. The goal is not to find a perfect apartment; it is to avoid problems that directly damage daily life.

## 1. Review Audit

- Read the newest low-star Google Maps, Yelp, ApartmentRatings, Reddit, and local review posts first.
- Search within reviews for: `break-in`, `stolen`, `garage`, `package`, `mail`, `noise`, `thin walls`, `alarm`, `bug`, `roach`, `ant`, `mold`, `fee`, `move out`, `management`.
- Treat repeated recent patterns as stronger than aggregate ratings.
- If one site looks clean and another has repeated severe complaints, mark it `mixed / needs recheck`.

## 2. Map And Geography

- Check distance and exposure to Caltrain, BART, VTA, light rail, freight rail, El Camino, San Tomas, Saratoga Ave, Central Expressway, Capitol Expressway, `101`, `237`, `280`, `880`, SJC flight paths, Levi's Stadium, Great America, and public garages.
- Distinguish walkable city noise from hard noise sources. Restaurants and people can be acceptable; train horns, expressway racing, aircraft, and stadium events are different.
- Verify whether a building is truly set back or only marketed as quiet.

## 3. Building Structure

- Confirm whether it is true Type I concrete or podium/wood over concrete garage.
- For wood/podium buildings, prefer top floor by default.
- For high-rise downtown buildings, prefer `15+` floors and avoid street/public-garage-facing mid-low floors.
- Do not treat "luxury", "new", or "LEED" as proof of acoustic comfort.

## 4. Garage And Car Safety

- Prefer resident-only gated or underground parking with fob-controlled elevator access.
- Look for review patterns around tailgating, broken garage gates, car break-ins, stolen catalytic converters, storage-room theft, and management response.
- Large communities and short-term/corporate rental turnover increase garage-door openings and stranger traffic.

## 5. Package And Food Delivery

- Prefer Luxer One, Amazon Hub, monitored package rooms, or concierge-controlled package handling.
- Downgrade lobby piles, hallway delivery, damaged mailboxes, package-room crowding, and "management cannot help" patterns.
- For Uber Eats or food delivery, check whether drivers can legally stop, find the entrance, and leave food in an indoor monitored area.

## 6. Unit Placement

- Avoid units near elevators, trash rooms, loading docks, garage gates, garage ramps, mechanical rooms, dog runs, pools, gyms, BBQ/fire pits, rooftops, and main pedestrian paths.
- Courtyard-facing is useful only if the courtyard is not an amenity echo chamber.
- Prioritize open view, privacy, and shielded exposure over compass direction alone.

## 7. Decision Labels

- `主推 / top pick`: strong fit, still verify current availability and exact unit.
- `条件保留 / conditional`: works only under exact unit conditions.
- `备选 / backup`: acceptable if stronger options fail.
- `不推荐 / not recommended`: mismatches important preferences.
- `淘汰 / eliminate`: hits core red flags.
