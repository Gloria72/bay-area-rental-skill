## Strong Or Current Shortlist Candidates

### Cobalt Apartments, Santa Clara

Default status: `主推 / first Santa Clara candidate`.

Why: 2016-era newer feel, `1B` around `709/752/772 sqft`, in-unit washer/dryer, package service, controlled/gated access, night patrol, EV charging, and fewer obvious garage/package/security review red flags than many Santa Clara/North SJ new builds.

Conditions: only high-floor, interior/shielded `700+ sqft 1B`; avoid Saratoga Ave, main-road exposure, rooftop/pool/BBQ, and garage gate locations.

### Passero, Santa Clara

Default status: `条件保留 / new-build backup`.

Why: 2025 Greystar new community with smart-home features, Samsung appliances, in-home washer/dryer, dedicated A/C, and strong new-building feel.

Risks: too new, short or possibly honeymoon/incentivized review sample, Greystar caution, and Great America / Levi's / 237 / SJC activity and traffic/noise zone. Rank behind Cobalt unless current reviews stay clean and exact unit is shielded.

### 1250 Lakeside

Default status: `条件保留`, not baseline.

Why: newer feel, lake/trail lifestyle, floor-to-ceiling window appeal, in-home washer/dryer, package lockers, modern finishes, and strong lifestyle fit when unit placement is good.

Risks from notes: recent review split; upstairs footsteps/thin floors, management response, parking permit/towing confusion, garage gate/security issues, possible 101/construction noise, and tree/light obstruction.

Only consider: top floor or very strong high-floor unit; interior/lake/courtyard-facing; not toward 101, driveway, construction, garage gate, or heavy tree shade. Non-top-floor units in this building should be downgraded.

Known unit reasoning from source notes: `C430 > C207`. `C430` was judged better because 4th floor, more open, likely east/northeast, brighter, more private, and farther from central amenity noise. `C207` was acceptable but lower, more inner-facing, more compressed, likely north/northwest and dimmer.

### Maxwell Sunnyvale

Default status: `主推 if correct unit`.

Why: good match for new-feeling `1B` around `718 sqft`, in-unit laundry, A/C, controlled access, package lockers, on-site management/maintenance, night patrol, double-pane windows.

Conditions: high-floor, courtyard-facing, not toward S Mathilda Ave, W McKinley Ave, tracks, garage gate, elevator, trash room, or amenity courtyard. Source note treated unit `318` as a backup if it faces Mathilda and is not top floor.

### ARLO Mountain View

Default status: `条件保留`.

Why: new-feeling, strong finishes and amenities, front-loading washer/dryer, smart-home features, controlled/gated access.

Risks: Castro / El Camino / Downtown Mountain View / Caltrain-VTA convenience is a double-edged sword for a light sleeper. Only high-floor courtyard/back-facing units away from Castro, El Camino, rail, and amenity fire pit.

### Landsby

Risks: close to San Antonio Caltrain; `1B` may exceed budget, so studio is more realistic. Only high-floor interior units away from Caltrain, Central Expressway, San Antonio Rd, rooftop, pool, and lounge.

### Loft House / The Flats / The Martin

Risks: downtown Sunnyvale, Caltrain, restaurants, events, retail, and street activity. Do not confuse acceptable city liveliness with hard noise. Choose high-floor interior units away from retail, rail, amenity zones, and main roads.
